NEWLY VERIFIED UNIFORM ESTIMATES
Write M=100n, C*=361/200, s=i/M, and zeta(y)=1+(3/5+I)y.
All estimates refer to the actual analytic functions and concrete basis.

1. Factorials and normalization.
   The Stirling remainder log(m!)-(m+1/2)log(m)+m lies in [11/12,1]
   for m>=1. The zero case is covered by a separate uniform main-term bound.
   After the explicit entropy and constant correction are retained, the
   target factorial normalization has absolute error at most 5/6 for n>=1.
   The basis factorial row rate is r(s)=-4s log(2s)+4s, continuous also at 0.

2. Root products and half-unit contour shift.
   The centered root-product logarithm differs from its root-log integral
   by an error between the two endpoint logarithms. Scaling is exact.
   The potential change under the half-step shift is bounded uniformly,
   including arbitrarily small positive y and the unbounded contour.
   Consequently, for an explicit constant Cw independent of n and y,
     log|w_n(gamma_n(My))| <= M G(y) - (13/2)log M
                              + Cw + (17/2)log(1+y).
   The actual 45-center basis has the uniform bound
     2 log|q_i(gamma_n(My)^2)| <= 4i log M - 2log((2i)!)
         + i P_s(zeta(y)^2) + Cb + 360log(1+y)
   whenever that basis value is nonzero; the norm/density bounds include zeros.

3. Uniform contour integration.
   A common integrable majorant, with polynomial growth times exp(-2*pi*y),
   controls every index. Lower and upper energies are equal by the proved
   conjugation identities. If the numerical envelope V satisfies the
   pointwise certificate and has a fixed lower bound, then one constant C
   works simultaneously for all n>=1 and all admissible i:
     Energy(n,i) <= C M exp(M*(V(i/M)+r(i/M))).
   No Laplace asymptotic or unproved integral bound is assumed.

4. Selected minors, basis transfer and row sums.
   The loss from omitting four indices is bounded explicitly. The factorial
   minor prefactor, the contour-size prefactor, and the exact triangular
   basis transfer all have logarithms negligible after division by n^2.
   The actual floor-sized row sum converges to the integral of every
   continuous envelope plus r. A direct quadrature-error proof handles the
   moving last grid point and the extra endpoint term.
   The factorial contribution integrates exactly to
     integral_0^C* r(s) ds = -2(C*)^2 log(2C*) + 3(C*)^2.

5. Combined theorem for the ACTUAL analytic square-basis zeta matrix.
   Given a continuous envelope V on [0,C*], a lower bound, and the numerical
   pointwise certificate, Lean proves the existence of a single sequence E
   with E(n)/n^2 -> 0 such that EVERY codimension-four selected minor satisfies
     |det| <= exp(M^2 A(V) + E(n)),
     A(V) = integral_0^C* V(s) ds - 2(C*)^2 log(2C*) + 3(C*)^2.
   The estimate is uniform over the row and column selections. It includes
   the exact change from the adapted basis to the analytic square basis.
