Four Odd Zeta Manuscript — revised 15 October 2026

This package contains the editable LaTeX manuscript, its compiled PDF, exact
polynomial witness tables, bibliography, and the completed Round 29 report.
Compile Four_Odd_Zeta_Manuscript.tex with pdflatex until references stabilize
(normally two or three passes). Standard TeX Live packages suffice.

This edition incorporates the final arithmetic lower rate -11.52, the analytic
upper rate -11.55706678, their gap 0.03706678, the final contradiction, and the
completed audit. Eight finite numerical admissions remain in the supplied
Lean conclusion; the explicit-certificate deduction is admission-free.

The separate Four_Value_Lean_Round29.zip contains the proof sources, numerical
certificates, and reproduction scripts. It is not duplicated in this package.
See Appendix B and the included final report for exact reproduction commands.
The historical references include publication links; no authorship is assigned
to this manuscript by this revision.
